## Summary

This paper proposes 'Hypersexual Disorder' as a new psychiatric diagnosis for the DSM-5 to address repetitive, intense sexual preoccupation and behavior. The author synthesizes existing literature to define the condition as a non-paraphilic sexual desire disorder involving impulsivity. Proposed diagnostic criteria include specific behavioral specifiers meant to integrate perspectives on sexual addiction, compulsivity, and arousal dysregulation. The study emphasizes that these behaviors often lead to clinical distress and social or medical morbidity. The primary takeaway is the recommendation for a standardized, criterion-based clinical definition to categorize individuals suffering from out-of-control sexual behaviors.
