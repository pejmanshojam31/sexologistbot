## Summary

This study examines the motivations and professional experiences of women working as adult webcam models in Türkiye. Based on qualitative interviews with 12 participants, researchers explored how these workers navigate the intersection of financial needs, emotional labor, and societal stigma. The findings indicate that webcam modeling serves as both a financial necessity and a venue for addressing emotional needs. Models employ adaptive strategies to manage platform requirements, gendered societal expectations, and the risks of public exposure. The study concludes that such labor involves complex, context-specific negotiations in morally conservative and legally ambiguous environments. Ultimately, the authors advocate for policies that recognize the agency and rights of webcam workers.
